# Community pages: research and sources

Compiled October 11, 2026 for the 24 pages under /service-area/{slug}/. Every local fact on those pages traces to a source below. Data lives in src/data/locations.mjs.

## Method

- **Permit offices** were confirmed on each municipality's official website (one exception noted under LaSalle).
- **Coordinates** come from the US Census 2023 Gazetteer (place or county-subdivision internal points). They are used only for the "about N miles from Monroe (straight line)" figure and the nearby-community links.
- **Geography and history** come from official pages where possible, otherwise Wikipedia. Wikipedia facts are used only for stable geography, never for rules.
- **No local rule is published** unless it was found on an official site. No deck permit thresholds (sq ft, 30 in.) are published except Maumee's, which come from the city's own form.

## Not published (unverified)

- Toledo deck-permit size/height thresholds (third-party sites only).
- An Ottawa Hills architectural review board (not found on the village site).
- Oak Openings soils for Holland (not verified for the village itself).
- Luna Pier fence numbers: Ordinance 153 dates from about 2007 and may be superseded by Zoning Ordinance 211 (2024), so the page describes the limits in general terms and says the current ordinance is confirmed per lot.
- Monroe County does not issue building permits for any of the 24 communities; each has its own department, or uses the county in Ohio.

## Communities in the two counties without a page yet

The 24 pages match the communities listed on the service-area page. Incorporated places not yet covered: Estral Beach (village, Berlin Charter Township), Milan and Flat Rock (cities partly in Monroe County), and Harbor View and Berkey (villages, Lucas County). Townships without their own page include Raisinville, London, Milan, Exeter, Ash, Berlin, Summerfield, Dundee and Whiteford in Monroe County, and Sylvania, Springfield, Monclova, Washington, Jerusalem, Waterville, Spencer, Harding, Providence, Richfield and Swanton in Lucas County. Each needs the same verified research before a page is built.
